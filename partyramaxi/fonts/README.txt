Fonts for PartyramaXI (party list, target bar and RecastXI).

Bundled (SIL Open Font License 1.1, licenses in licenses\): Barlow Semi Condensed, Cinzel, Exo 2, Fredoka,
Inter, JetBrains Mono, Marcellus SC, Nunito ExtraBold, Rubik (Regular and Bold).
Rubik and Nunito ExtraBold are static instances made from the Google Fonts variable files.

Add your own: put .ttf or .otf files here, then press Rescan in /pxi config (or /addon reload partyramaxi).
They appear in every font drop-down.

Use STATIC files, e.g. Nunito-Regular.ttf and Nunito-Bold.ttf (from the "static" folder of a Google Fonts download).
Variable fonts (names with [wght]) often fail to load in ImGui.
